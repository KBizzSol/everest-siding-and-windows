6 Reasons Why Brick Waterproofing Fails

Dec 30, 2025

Brick waterproofing helps reduce water absorption, but it works best when combined with proper drainage, flashing, and wall maintenance. Many homeowners apply sealers expecting long-term results, but problems like peeling, cracking, or moisture stains often show up sooner than expected.

In most cases, failure happens because of poor preparation, wrong products, or hidden structural issues. Read this blog to learn why brick waterproofing fails. This will help you avoid costly repairs and extend the life of your masonry.

1. Poor Surface Preparation

One of the biggest causes of failure is improper cleaning. Dirt, dust, grease, mold, or old coatings prevent sealers from bonding to the brick surface.

If bricks are not thoroughly cleaned—using low-pressure washing or approved brick cleaners—the waterproofing layer will peel or wash off during rain. Trapped moisture can cause blistering or surface damage when temperatures rise.

Always clean and dry bricks fully and test a small area before sealing the entire wall.

2. Using the Wrong Waterproofing Product

Not all brick sealers work the same way. Some products are not suitable for porous masonry.

Non-breathable sealers trap moisture inside the brick. This leads to:

  • Cracking
  • Flaking
  • Efflorescence (white powdery stains)
  • Brick spalling

Low-quality or improperly applied sealers may break down faster when exposed to sun and weather. Others react poorly with certain mortars or paints. The best choice is a penetrating, breathable sealer such as silane or siloxane made for exterior brick.

3. Incorrect Application Methods

Even the right product can fail if it’s applied incorrectly.

Common mistakes include:

  • Applying sealers in wet, hot, or windy weather.
  • Applying too much product at once or sealing damp brick can prevent proper absorption.
  • Not allowing proper curing time between coats.
  • Over-applying the product to damp bricks.

4. Ignoring Mortar and Structural Problems

Sealants do not fix already damaged brick walls. If you are scheduling waterproofing services and want them to work, you must first fix damaged parts such as cracked bricks, loose mortar joints or poor foundation. This will allow the waterproofing material to set and avoid problems later on.

5. Environmental and Maintenance Issues

Maintenance issues, combined with environmental factors like clogged gutters, high soil levels, poor drainage, and prolonged exposure to water or sunlight, can cause waterproofing to fail. Most products need reapplication every 5–10 years, especially in harsh climates with heavy rain or freezing temperatures.

6. Design and Drainage Flaws

If the drainage system in your house doesn’t work properly, water can pool around brick walls. Poor drainage can be caused by missing flashing, poor landscaping, and leaking downspouts. This can lead to the failure of waterproofing as water can pool and build moisture through tiny cracks.

How to Prevent Brick Waterproofing Failure

Here is how you can prevent brick waterproofing issues:

  • Fix drainage and moisture issues first
  • Repair cracks and mortar joints
  • Use breathable, masonry-safe sealers
  • Apply in proper weather conditions
  • Perform regular inspections

Final Thoughts

Brick waterproofing can fail for several reasons, including poor surface preparation, using the wrong product, ignoring repairs, maintenance issues, design flaws, and incorrect application.
