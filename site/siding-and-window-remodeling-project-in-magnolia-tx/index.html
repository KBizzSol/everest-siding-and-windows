Siding and Window Remodeling Project in Magnolia, TX



When

August 2023


Project

Siding and Window Replacement


Product

LP SmartSide board and batten style siding. NT Twinsulator aluminum windows, black in and black out.


How much

$78,418

Problem:

This charming two-story home in Magnolia was due for a siding update, and the homeowners seized the opportunity to enhance their windows as well. Their vision was to create a more energy-efficient home with a fresh, modern aesthetic that still honored the traditional character of the house.

Our Solution:

The siding was upgraded to board-and-batten style LP SmartSide engineered wood, creating a fresh look for the home. Both the new siding and existing brick were painted in the soft, timeless shade of Oyster White (SW 7637). Additionally, the old windows were replaced with black NT Twinsulator aluminum windows, which added a striking contrast against the white siding. This combination achieves a clean, contemporary style while preserving a classic feel, thanks to the traditional board-and-batten design.
