    HardiePlank Products

    HardiePlank products are popular for many reasons, including the following:

    Moisture and Rot Resistant

    Natural wood siding has a tendency to expand, buckle, crack, and mold when repeatedly exposed to precipitation. Vinyl siding installations may put you at risk for water intrusion, especially around windows and trim joints. James Hardie products are specifically engineered to withstand damage from moisture and rot. They will hold their own in rain, sleet, or snow.

    Durable over Time

    One thing’s for sure: As the years go on, we all show signs of aging. However, since James Hardie products are created to stand up to the demands of the specific climate in Houston, they will look great for years, saving you time and money. With James Hardie’s added benefit of ColorPlus Technology—a baked-on color option that resists fading—your siding will look great without excessive maintenance.

    Offer Good Fire Protection

    In case of a fire, you want to know that the walls of your house will protect you while your family seeks safety. Fire feasts on wood, and it melts vinyl almost instantly. But James Hardie’s siding will not ignite when exposed to a direct flame, nor will it contribute fuel to a fire. This is because James Hardie’s products are non-combustible, and many insurance companies offer a discount.

    Unsusceptible to Pests

    Wood siding can be pecked by birds and devoured by insects. James Hardie fiber cement siding in Houston offers little appeal to critters and won’t be eaten by termites. You can focus on the animals in your life that actually make you happy, not on those that cause distress and damage.

    Hardie Plank ColorPlus Technology

    ColorPlus Technology is James Hardie’s proprietary finish applied inside a controlled factory setting to help eliminate debris and optimize adhesion to give you a lower maintenance solution. The finish is cured in between coats in a controlled environment for an even stronger bond that resists chipping, peeling and cracking. ColorPlus Technology resists fading and looks good for longer.

    ColorPlus Technology finish comes with a 15-year limited warranty that covers paint and labor, protecting against peeling, cracking, and chipping.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Hardie Plank a good choice for Houston weather?

    Yes. Hardie Plank fiber cement is Engineered for Climate — it resists moisture and rot, is non-combustible for added fire protection, and won’t be eaten by termites, making it well suited to Houston’s heat and humidity.

    What warranty does Hardie Plank include?

    Hardie Plank products carry a 30-year limited product warranty, and the ColorPlus Technology finish is covered by a 15-year limited warranty against peeling, cracking, and chipping. Your installation also includes Everest’s Lifetime Labor Warranty.

    What styles and colors are available?

    You can choose lap, vertical panel, shingle (shake), trim, and soffit profiles in cedar texture or smooth finishes — either primed for paint or pre-finished with baked-on ColorPlus color that resists fading.
