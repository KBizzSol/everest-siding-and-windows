Siding and Window Remodeling Project in Kingwood, TX



When

October 2020


Project

Siding Replacement


Product

LP SmartSide 8” lap siding with Hardie Plank soffit and fascia


How much

$20,980

Problem:

The siding on this charming property in Kingwood was getting old and started to fail, so it needed to be replaced and brought back to standard.

Our Solution:

The customer decided to use LP 8” lap siding on the house and LP board and batten style for the garage, with Hardie Plank for the soffit and fascia. All new siding was painted with Emerald Sherwin Williams Cadet (SW 9143) color and accented with Pure White (SW 7005) color. All brick was also cleaned, primed, and painted with the Cadet color.
