How Long Does It Take To Pressure Wash A House?

Nov 15, 2024

If you want to deep clean your house, pressure washing can indeed be a great way to do that. Hiring a professional pressure washing service in Houston can provide great value for money in terms of quick, effective and reliable work.

However, one thing that remains tentative is how long does it take to pressure wash a home? If your time is of great priority to you – learn about these key factors which may influence your decision!

How Long Will It Take To Pressure Wash My House?

Pressure washing a house may seem a simple and quick task but there is a lot to consider! The amount of time it takes to pressure wash a house is dependent on various factors such as:

  • Size of your home
  • Last you cleaned your house professionally
  • Is there algae or dirt accumulated?
  • Are there any hard-to-reach areas?
  • Equipment being used
  • Experience of your hired services
  • Additional services

Factors That Affect The Total Time Required For Pressure Washing

We will build on each of these factors below in detail!

Size Of Your House

The size of your house is a primary factor which may affect the total time needed to pressure wash. Many professional cleaning companies regard the total square foot of your house to determine the total length of a pressure washing project. Ideally, homes in the range of 155-2500 sq ft may take 1-6 hours to complete while bigger houses of 3500 sq ft and more may take up to 8 hours to completely clean.

Dirt Accumulated

Another important factor which can signify the total number of hours it may take to complete pressure washing is the amount of dirt accumulated. While estimating the total duration, professionals take into account whether your house has algae or dirt. Comparatively, a cleaner house may require less time to pressure wash.

Equipment Used

The type of equipment being used is the largest contributing factor to determine the total duration of pressure washing. Professionals who use large, contractor grade pressure washing equipment may take less time as compared to those who use small electric equipment.

Additional Services

During the pressure washing, if you request your service provider to clean the driveway, patio, deck or fence – it may add-on to the total number of hours required to complete the project.

Experience of Hired Professionals

An experienced technician may give similar results far quicker than a novice. So, if you are hiring a considerably less experienced service, make sure to log in extra time to complete the project.

Final Words

Pressure washing is great if you are considering a deep clean for your house. However, the time for each project remains tentative. The total duration might depend on the size of your house, dirt accumulated, equipment used, additional services and experience of hired professionals.
